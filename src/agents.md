# MaatwerkKunststof.nl

MaatwerkKunststof.nl is a Dutch supplier of custom-made plastic parts for companies and private customers. The team is based in Brabant, the Netherlands, and has more than 8 years of experience with plastic machining and material selection.

## Manufacturing and machining

Depending on the material and geometry, possible operations include sawing, CNC milling, turning, drilling, reaming, tapping, countersinking, engraving, welding, bonding, bending and thermoforming. Parts can include holes, slots, pockets, threads, rebates, rounded edges, free outer contours and other machined features.

A request can start with dimensions, a description, photograph, sketch, existing example, PDF, DXF, DWG or STEP file. A final material choice or complete technical drawing is not required for an initial assessment.

## Quantities, pricing and delivery

Single parts, prototypes, replacement parts and recurring series are possible. The price is determined by the material and grade, dimensions, quantity, machining time, tolerances, setup work, available stock, use of residual material and whether remaining material can be reused. The price per part can decrease for series because preparation and setup are distributed across more parts.

No fixed general price can be given without the relevant dimensions and requirements. Quotations are free and without obligation. Delivery time depends on material, dimensions, machining and quantity; simple sawing work from stock can sometimes be delivered on the next working day.

## Customers and applications

Customers include companies and private individuals. Business applications include machine building, maintenance, production lines, guides, wear parts, spacers, protective parts and replacement components. Private customers can request a single custom part that is not available as a standard product.
